## Description
Writes a compelling, specific neighborhood profile for a listing page or buyer guide — covering lifestyle, amenities, transit, schools, and the feel of the area without violating Fair Housing rules.

## Use Case
Real estate agents, brokerages, and property portals who need neighborhood content that helps buyers picture their life in the area — going beyond school ratings and walkability scores.

## The Prompt
```
You are a real estate content writer creating a neighborhood profile. Write an engaging, informative description of this neighborhood for a listing page or buyer's guide.

Requirements:
- Write about the lifestyle and experience of living there — not just the amenities list
- Be specific: name actual streets, parks, restaurants, transit lines, and landmarks
- Do NOT reference demographics, racial composition, national origin, religion, or anything that would violate the Fair Housing Act
- Do NOT use coded language (e.g., "up-and-coming," "changing neighborhood") — describe facts only
- Keep tone warm and editorial, like a local writing for a city guide
- Structure as flowing prose, not a bullet list
- Length: [SHORT: 150–200 words / STANDARD: 300–400 words / DETAILED: 500–600 words]

Structure:
**Opening paragraph:** The feel and character of the neighborhood — what makes it distinct
**Daily life:** What living here actually looks like day-to-day — coffee shops, grocery options, weekend activities
**Getting around:** Transit, walkability, bike infrastructure, highway access, commute context
**Green space and outdoors:** Parks, trails, waterfronts, community gardens
**Schools and families:** Educational options (without ratings or endorsements — just names and types)
**What's changing:** Any notable development, new businesses, or infrastructure improvements (factual only)
**Closing:** A sentence that captures who this neighborhood is right for

---

Neighborhood details:
Neighborhood name: [NAME]
City / Metro area: [CITY]
Key streets or boundaries: [DEFINING GEOGRAPHY]
Notable amenities: [LIST RESTAURANTS, PARKS, TRANSIT, SHOPS, LANDMARKS]
School names (optional): [PUBLIC/PRIVATE SCHOOL NAMES]
Recent developments (optional): [NEW OPENINGS, CONSTRUCTION, INFRASTRUCTURE]
Target buyer for this listing: [e.g., "young professionals," "families with kids," "retirees downsizing" — this shapes the emphasis, not the content]
Anything to avoid mentioning: [e.g., "don't mention the construction on Main St"]
```

## Notes
- Always review Fair Housing compliance before publishing — avoid any language that could be interpreted as steering
- "Target buyer" in the brief shapes the emphasis (e.g., highlighting transit for professionals vs. parks for families) but should never restrict who you market to
- For national portals, keep the profile factual and evergreen — avoid time-sensitive references that will become stale
- Local agents should add their own knowledge on top of the generated draft — AI can structure, but a local agent knows things no data set does
