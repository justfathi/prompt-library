## Description
Generates a coordinated set of launch copy — press release, launch email, and social media posts — for a new product or feature, all aligned in tone and key messaging.

## Use Case
Product marketers, founders, and PR teams who need to ship a complete launch communication package without coordinating five different copy briefs across five different writers.

## The Prompt
```
You are a product marketing writer. Using the launch brief below, write a complete set of coordinated launch assets.

---

**ASSET 1: PRESS RELEASE**

[COMPANY NAME] FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Headline: [Newsworthy, specific, no hype — what happened and why it matters]
Subheadline: [One sentence expanding the headline]

[City, Date] — [Opening paragraph: the who, what, when, where, why — most important info first, 2–3 sentences]

[Second paragraph: how the product works and what problem it solves — include a specific stat or proof point if available]

[Quote from a company spokesperson — make it human, not corporate boilerplate]

[Third paragraph: availability, pricing, and where to learn more]

About [COMPANY NAME]: [2-sentence boilerplate]
Contact: [NAME] | [EMAIL] | [PHONE]

---

**ASSET 2: LAUNCH EMAIL**
Subject line: (2 options)
Preview text: (max 90 characters)
Body: (under 200 words — lead with the benefit, not the feature. One CTA.)

---

**ASSET 3: SOCIAL MEDIA POSTS**
LinkedIn post: (150–300 words — professional tone, can be more narrative)
Twitter/X thread: (4–6 tweets — punchy, each tweet stands alone, thread builds on itself)
Instagram caption: (conversational, 2–3 short paragraphs, end with a question or CTA, 5 relevant hashtags)

---

**ASSET 4: ONE-LINER (for team updates, Slack, email signatures)**
[One sentence that explains the product and its value — under 20 words]

---

Launch brief:
Product name: [NAME]
What it is: [PLAIN ENGLISH DESCRIPTION]
What problem it solves: [THE PAIN IT FIXES]
Who it's for: [TARGET CUSTOMER]
Key differentiator: [WHY THIS AND NOT SOMETHING ELSE]
Price / availability: [PRICING MODEL AND LAUNCH DATE]
One stat or proof point (optional): [DATA POINT, BETA RESULT, OR CUSTOMER QUOTE]
Brand tone: [TONE GUIDANCE]
```

## Notes
- The one-liner (Asset 4) is often the hardest to write but the most reused — iterate on it first
- Press release quotes should sound like a real person said them, not a corporate communications team
- Social posts should not just copy each other — each platform has different norms; this prompt handles that
- For B2B products, swap the Instagram post for a LinkedIn carousel outline
