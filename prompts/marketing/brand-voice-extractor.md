## Description
Analyzes sample brand content and produces a reusable brand voice and tone guide — including vocabulary rules, sentence style, and "do / don't" examples — that any writer or AI tool can follow.

## Use Case
Brand strategists, content leads, and marketing teams who want to codify their voice so that content stays consistent across writers, channels, and tools.

## The Prompt
```
You are a brand strategist specializing in voice and tone. Analyze the sample content below and produce a comprehensive brand voice guide.

Structure the guide as follows:

---

**BRAND VOICE GUIDE**
Brand: [BRAND NAME]

**1. Voice in 3 Words**
[Pick 3 adjectives that define the brand voice — e.g., "Bold. Warm. Direct."]
For each word, write one sentence explaining what it means in practice.

**2. The Brand Sounds Like**
[Write 3 short example sentences that perfectly capture the voice]

**3. The Brand Does NOT Sound Like**
[Write 3 short example sentences showing the wrong tone to avoid]

**4. Vocabulary Rules**
Words and phrases we use: [List 8–10 preferred terms]
Words and phrases we avoid: [List 8–10 words that feel off-brand — include corporate jargon, overused buzzwords, or anything that clashes with the personality]

**5. Sentence & Structural Style**
[Describe: sentence length preference, use of contractions, punctuation style, use of humor, use of rhetorical questions, paragraph length, use of lists vs. prose]

**6. Tone Variations by Context**
[For each channel, note any tone adjustments:]
- Website copy:
- Email:
- Social media:
- Customer support:
- Error messages / UX microcopy:

**7. Writing Rules (Do / Don't)**
[5 specific rules with a do and don't example for each]

---

Analyze these samples and produce the guide above. Pull specific evidence from the samples to justify each element of the guide.

Sample content to analyze:
[PASTE 3–5 PIECES OF BRAND CONTENT HERE — website copy, emails, social posts, ads, etc.]

Brand context (optional):
Industry: [INDUSTRY]
Target audience: [WHO THEY WRITE FOR]
Positioning: [HOW THE BRAND SEES ITSELF]
```

## Notes
- More samples = more accurate guide — aim for at least 500 words of sample content spread across different content types
- Include both high-performing and "just okay" samples if available; the model can identify what made the strong pieces work
- The output works as a prompt prefix: paste the voice guide at the top of any writing prompt to keep AI-generated content on-brand
- Revisit the guide every 12–18 months or after a brand refresh
