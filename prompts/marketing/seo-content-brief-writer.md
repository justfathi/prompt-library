## Description
Generates a detailed SEO content brief for a target keyword — including search intent analysis, recommended structure, semantic keywords, internal linking suggestions, and competitive differentiation notes.

## Use Case
SEO strategists, content managers, and freelance writers who need a structured brief to guide the creation of a page designed to rank — without spending hours on manual research synthesis.

## The Prompt
```
You are an SEO content strategist. Create a comprehensive content brief for the target keyword below.

**SEO CONTENT BRIEF**

**Target Keyword:** [PRIMARY KEYWORD]
**Secondary Keywords:** [LIST ANY KNOWN RELATED TERMS — or ask the model to suggest them]
**Target URL slug:** [SUGGESTED URL]
**Content type:** [Blog post / Landing page / Comparison page / Hub page]

---

**1. Search Intent Analysis**
- Primary intent: [Informational / Navigational / Commercial / Transactional]
- What the searcher actually wants to accomplish: [1–2 sentences]
- What the page must deliver to satisfy this intent: [Specific content requirements]

**2. Recommended Title Tag**
[Write 2 options — include primary keyword, keep under 60 characters]

**3. Recommended Meta Description**
[Write 2 options — include primary keyword, include a benefit or hook, keep under 155 characters]

**4. Suggested H1**
[Different from the title tag but aligned — can be longer and more descriptive]

**5. Recommended Page Structure**
[List the H2s and H3s — this is the backbone of the article. For each H2, write a 1-sentence note on what it should cover and why]

**6. Semantic Keywords to Include**
[List 15–20 related terms, LSI keywords, and entity names that should appear naturally in the content]

**7. Word Count Recommendation**
[Suggested range based on the topic complexity and competitor depth — explain the reasoning]

**8. Content Differentiation**
[3 specific ways this page can be better or different from what currently ranks — e.g., original data, a unique angle, a comparison table, a decision tool, deeper examples]

**9. Internal Linking Opportunities**
[Suggest 3–5 topic areas or existing content types this page should link to and receive links from]

**10. Schema Markup Recommendation**
[Suggest the most relevant schema type: Article / FAQ / HowTo / Product / Review / etc. — and explain why]

**11. Call to Action**
[What should the reader do at the end of the page — be specific about the offer and the CTA placement]

---

Keyword and context:
Primary keyword: [KEYWORD]
Website/brand: [BRAND OR SITE NAME]
Target audience: [WHO THIS PAGE IS FOR]
Business goal: [WHAT THIS PAGE SHOULD DRIVE — leads, sales, newsletter signups, etc.]
Competitor URLs to beat (optional): [PASTE 1–3 COMPETING URLs]
```

## Notes
- Run this brief before writing — it saves significant rewrite time
- The semantic keywords section is especially valuable as a checklist during writing and editing
- For high-competition keywords, the differentiation section is the most important — focus on what the top results don't cover
- Pair with a writing prompt that includes this brief for a fully AI-drafted SEO article
