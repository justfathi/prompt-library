## Description
Drafts a complete, balanced Non-Disclosure Agreement (mutual or one-way) from key parameters — covering confidential information definition, obligations, exceptions, term, and remedies.

## Use Case
Founders, consultants, and business development teams who need a solid NDA quickly for a partnership discussion, vendor engagement, or hiring conversation — before involving legal counsel for finalization.

## The Prompt
```
You are a business attorney drafting a Non-Disclosure Agreement. Using the parameters below, draft a complete NDA.

The agreement should include:

1. **Parties** — full legal names and relationship
2. **Purpose** — why confidential information is being shared
3. **Definition of Confidential Information** — what is covered (broad but standard)
4. **Exclusions** — what is NOT confidential (publicly known, independently developed, received from third parties, required by law)
5. **Obligations of Receiving Party** — standard of care, use restrictions, need-to-know sharing
6. **Term** — duration of the agreement and survival of confidentiality obligations post-termination
7. **Return or Destruction of Information** — what happens to confidential materials at end of engagement
8. **No License** — no IP rights granted by disclosure
9. **Remedies** — acknowledgment that breach causes irreparable harm; right to seek injunctive relief
10. **General Provisions** — governing law, entire agreement, amendment, severability, no waiver

Format as a clean, ready-to-sign legal document with proper article and section numbering.

---

NDA parameters:
Type: [Mutual (both parties share confidential info) / One-Way (only one party discloses)]
Disclosing party: [FULL LEGAL NAME AND TYPE — e.g., "Acme Corp, a Delaware corporation"]
Receiving party: [FULL LEGAL NAME AND TYPE]
Purpose of disclosure: [e.g., "evaluation of a potential business partnership" / "software development engagement"]
Term of agreement: [e.g., "2 years from the Effective Date"]
Confidentiality survival period: [e.g., "obligations survive for 3 years after termination"]
Governing law: [STATE / JURISDICTION]
Any special provisions (optional): [e.g., "exclude employee non-solicitation" / "include specific carve-outs for open-source software"]
```

## Notes
- This draft is a starting point — have a licensed attorney in the relevant jurisdiction review before signing for any significant engagement
- Mutual NDAs are standard for most partnership discussions; one-way NDAs are appropriate when only one party is disclosing sensitive information
- The confidentiality survival period (after the agreement ends) is often overlooked — 2–5 years is standard
- For employee NDAs, additional jurisdiction-specific requirements apply (California, for example, has significant restrictions)
